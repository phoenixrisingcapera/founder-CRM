# AiStack Product Memory

This file is the working memory for the intended product direction.

## Canonical Product Name

AiStack

Do not call the product `Dojo` in the active CRM direction.

`Dojo` may only be used historically or as a legacy reference, not as the active product identity.

## Core Product Truth

The real core of the CRM is:

### Founder Relationship Mapping

Map investors, advisors, operators, and warm paths around specific venture goals.

The system should help founders:
- visualize relationships
- uncover valuable introductions
- understand network proximity
- turn relationship context into action

### Venture Workflow Intelligence

Organize companies, people, conversations, notes, and research into structured workflows that drive real venture outcomes.

This means the CRM is not just a database of contacts.
It is a workflow system that helps founders move from relationship context to venture execution.

### Controlled Backend Integration

The frontend is meant to be ready for private backend APIs and controlled intelligence layers, including:
- authentication
- LinkedIn data
- CRM records
- deterministic scoring
- future AI scoring
- relationship intelligence

## Product Framing

AiStack is a founder and venture workflow CRM.

It should be understood as:
- relationship mapping first
- workflow intelligence second
- deck and venture-material support as a connected module

It should not be understood as:
- a generic CRM
- a sales CRM
- a DDDecks clone
- a pure deck editor

## Core CRM Features

### Public Entry

AiStack should have a public entry point for founder and venture workflow orientation.

### Projects

Manage research-led projects, workspaces, and venture operating threads.

### Dispatches

Create outbound plans, follow-ups, and relationship actions.

### People

Track investors, advisors, operators, and key contacts.

### Companies

Organize target companies and strategic relationships.

### Opportunities

Track funding chances, partnerships, and growth opportunities.

### Relationship Graph

Visualize connections, warm paths, and network proximity.

### Goal-Based Fit Scoring

Use deterministic scoring around venture goals and signals.

## Core Workflow

### 1. Define the venture goal

Choose a clear goal such as:
- raise funding
- find advisors
- validate a market
- build an operator network

### 2. Map people and companies

Capture founders, investors, companies, operators, and relevant relationships in the CRM.

### 3. Score fit and proximity

Use deterministic fit scoring and proximity logic controlled by the AiStack backend.

### 4. Turn relationships into workflows

Create projects, dispatches, notes, contact paths, and CRM actions from the ranked relationship surface.

## Naming Guidance

Use these names in active product work:
- AiStack
- Founder CRM

- relationship graph
- dispatches

Avoid these names in active product framing:

## Immediate Product Implications

The app should evolve toward these primary surfaces:
- Dashboard
- Projects
- Dispatches
- People
- Companies
- Opportunities
- Relationship Graph
- Deck Assistant
- Settings
- Admin

The current contacts/investors/pipeline model is acceptable as an implementation bridge, but the long-term product language should move toward:
- people
- companies
- opportunities
- projects
- dispatches
- relationship graph
