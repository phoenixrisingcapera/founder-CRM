# Decision 0003: Founder Deck Assistant Boundary

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Deck support remains a module inside Founder CRM, but it is also a real founder product surface because creating investor-ready business pitch material is part of the fundraising workflow.

## In Scope

- upload founder decks
- parse slides
- critique narrative
- produce investor-specific recommendations
- save founder-owned generated artifacts
- prepare investor-ready materials from CRM context

## Out Of Scope

- full slide editor
- general presentation design tool
- collaborative canvas
- generic brand studio

## Why

The founder grill explicitly says the product should help founders create business pitches for fundraising, but without becoming a deck editor.

The later product correction also makes clear that deck support is not the product core. The product core is relationship mapping and venture workflow intelligence.
